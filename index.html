Exercise 5: TV energy and electricity prices

Three D3 charts on one page: a vertical bar chart with axes (5.1), a scatter plot that becomes a line chart (5.2) and a donut chart (5.3). Each chart has its own JavaScript file and its own div.

5.1 LED screens use the most energy at 55 inches

Average labelled energy use of 55 inch TVs by screen technology. Comparing one screen size removes the effect of size on energy use.

Data: Energy Rating TV registrations (Jan 2026), filtered to 55 inch models and averaged by screen type in KNIME.

5.2 Electricity spot prices have more than tripled since 1998

Average yearly spot price of electricity in Queensland, NSW, Victoria and South Australia. Each dot is one year; the line joins them.

Data: ARE_Spot_Prices.csv (Australian Energy Regulator, 1998–2024). Average excludes Tasmania and Snowy.

5.3 Over half of TV models are medium sized

Share of TV models by screen size category.

Data: Energy Rating TV registrations (Jan 2026), counted by screen size category in KNIME.
